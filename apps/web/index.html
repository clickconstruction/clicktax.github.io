ClickTax

Let your AI agent fill IRS forms, check the math, and hand you a printable PDF. Free forever. Nothing is stored.

Connect your agent

Add this remote MCP server to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any MCP client:

https://mcp.clicktax.org/mcp

In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the URL. No account, no key.

How it works

  1. Your agent asks the server for a form's schema and notes, then collects your numbers.
  2. The server validates the entries, computes the arithmetic lines (totals, self-employment tax, taxable income, tax), and returns the computed lines, warnings, and a link.
  3. The link opens here. Your browser fills the official IRS PDF locally and lets you download it.

Privacy by construction

  • The server keeps nothing: no database, no logs of what you send, no accounts.
  • Your filled-in values travel in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to any server.
  • The PDF is generated on your machine from the blank IRS form.

What it does not do

  • It does not e-file. Print, sign, and mail the return, or hand the PDF to a preparer.
  • It does not compute credits, itemized deductions, the qualified business income deduction, or the capital gains worksheet. It tells you when one of those applies.
  • It is not tax advice. Verify every line against the IRS instructions.

ClickTax is an open project by Click Construction. Forms are the official IRS PDFs for tax years 2024 and 2025.
